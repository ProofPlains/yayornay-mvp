A few practical questions

Restaurant feedback, made simple

Does the customer need to create an account?

No. Customers can scan, choose a rating and optionally comment without creating a FlashFeedback account.

Is the feedback public?

Feedback submitted through FlashFeedback is sent privately to the business. Any separate public review is the customer's choice; FlashFeedback doesn't control what they publish elsewhere.

Where should I put my restaurant feedback QR code?

Try tables, bill folders, counters, collection points, receipts or takeaway packaging. Choose a visible spot where customers have a natural moment to respond, then watch whether real feedback arrives.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Use printable signs, download your QR to add to your own materials or order available FlashFeedback stickers. Customers scan with their phone.

Is FlashFeedback a replacement for Google reviews?

No. Public reviews help people decide where to go. Private feedback helps you understand experiences and improve your restaurant. The two serve different purposes.
